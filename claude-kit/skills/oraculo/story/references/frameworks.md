# Frameworks Reference

Concise descriptions of each framework used in Story definition and when it applies.

---

## Double Diamond (Simplified)

The story session follows two brief movements: diverge slightly during reframing (explore the problem from different angles), then converge quickly to a focused definition. Unlike the full Epic exploration, the Story uses a compressed diamond — the divergence is narrow and purposeful, not expansive.

**When:** Always. Reframing is the brief diverge movement; the assumption check and gate drive convergence.

---

## Assumption Mapping (Simplified)

Makes the top 2-3 assumptions explicit and assesses their evidence level (high/medium/low). Unlike the full Epic version, the Story does not build a scored assumption register — it identifies and acknowledges the most critical assumptions without exhaustive scoring.

**When:** Assumption Check phase. Light focuses on feasibility assumptions (architecture fit, dependencies). Deep focuses on value assumptions (is this the right problem?).
